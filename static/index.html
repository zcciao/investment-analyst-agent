Glossary

How this works

The terms you'll see across the app.
The big idea
This isn't a stock picker. It's a plan tracker: you write down why you own a position, the app watches the world, and it only interrupts you when something would actually change your mind.
Core concepts
Plan
Your structured reason for holding a position (some apps call this a thesis). It includes a one-liner, a confidence score (1–10), a time horizon, the reasons that hold it up, and a when-to-exit set. Build a new one from You → + Add a plan.
Reason
A load-bearing claim that has to be true for your plan to work (some apps call this a pillar). Each reason has:
Current value — e.g., "Cloud growth 31% YoY"
Walk-away signal — the level at which the reason fails (e.g., "<25% for 2 quarters")
Data sources — what to monitor (10-Qs, earnings calls, indices, etc.)
Confidence
Your belief in the plan on a 1–10 scale (some apps call this conviction). Higher = stronger. The app uses this to weight portfolio-level numbers and to flag when you're adding to a low-confidence position.
Upcoming event
A known future event that could move the plan (some apps call this a catalyst) — earnings calls, product launches, regulatory decisions. They show up on Briefing as a heads-up before they happen.
Reason status
On track On track
The reason is holding clear of its walk-away signal. No action needed — this is the resting state. (Previously: "intact".)
At risk At risk
The reason is slipping toward its walk-away signal but hasn't crossed it yet. One more weak data point could break it. Worth a closer look — but you don't have to act yet. (Previously: "wobbling".)
Off track Off track
The reason has crossed its walk-away signal in the wrong direction. The "why" behind this position no longer holds — time to revisit the plan, revise the reason, or trigger your exit. (Previously: "broken".)
Ahead Ahead
The metric is materially better than expected — well clear of the failure condition and meaningfully improved vs. the recorded value. Consider raising the walk-away signal, raising confidence, or adding to the position. Reserved for genuine upside surprises, not routine performance. (Previously: "strengthening".)
Portfolio health
The Portfolio Health % on the Briefing screen is the share of all your reasons that are on track or ahead. 100% = nothing off-track. The ring turns amber below 80% and red below 50%.
The agents
🔭 Watcher
A background agent that continuously scans markets, social, news, and fundamentals for each reason's data sources. It surfaces candidates — it doesn't decide.
🔬 Analyst
The one you talk to on the Ask tab. Pulls live data, explains plan state, flags risk, and nudges you to revise when a reason goes off-track or ahead.
🩺 Reviewer
Triggered by you from the plan detail page (🔍 Review this plan). Pulls price history + fundamentals over the holding window, grades each reason (held up / failed / set too low / set too high / not enough data), and proposes concrete updates you can apply.
🛡️ Supervisor
A quality gate. Before any agent-proposed change touches your plans, the supervisor checks: is the evidence cited? is the reasoning sound? is the change warranted? Approve → committed. Reject → analyst tries again.
When to exit
Take profit
A target price or condition at which you'd realize gains. Set this when you build the plan so you don't have to decide in the heat of the moment.
Plan break
The condition that signals the plan is invalidated — usually expressed as "if reason X fails, I exit." This is your "stop loss" tied to logic, not price.
Time stop
A deadline at which you re-evaluate if the plan hasn't played out. Prevents money sitting in a stalled position forever.
