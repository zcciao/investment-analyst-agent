Glossary

How this works

The terms you'll see across the app.
The big idea
This isn't a stock picker. It's a thesis tracker: you write down why you own a position, the app watches the world, and it only interrupts you when something would actually change your mind.
Core concepts
Thesis
Your structured reason for holding a position. It includes a one-liner, a conviction score (1–10), a time horizon, the pillars that hold it up, and an exit plan. Build a new one from You → + Build new thesis.
Pillar
A load-bearing claim that has to be true for your thesis to work. Each pillar has:
Current value — e.g., "Cloud growth 31% YoY"
Threshold — the level at which the pillar breaks (e.g., "<25% for 2 quarters")
Watch signals — data sources monitored for changes (10-Qs, earnings calls, etc.)
Conviction
Your confidence in the thesis on a 1–10 scale. Higher = stronger belief. The app uses this to weight portfolio-level numbers and to flag when you're adding to a low-conviction position.
Catalyst
A known upcoming event that could move the thesis — earnings calls, product launches, regulatory decisions. They show up on Briefing as a heads-up before they happen.
Pillar status
Intact Intact
The pillar is holding above its threshold. No action needed — this is the resting state.
Wobbling Wobbling
The pillar is slipping toward its threshold but hasn't crossed it yet. One more weak data point could break it. Worth a closer look — but you don't have to act yet.
Broken Broken
The pillar has crossed its threshold in the wrong direction. The reason you bought this no longer holds — time to revisit the thesis, revise the pillar, or trigger your exit plan.
Strengthening Strengthening
The metric is materially better than expected — far from the failure condition and meaningfully improved vs. the recorded value. Consider raising the threshold, raising your conviction, or adding to the position. Reserved for genuine upside surprises, not routine performance.
Portfolio health
The Portfolio Health % on the Briefing screen is the share of all your pillars that are intact. 100% = nothing broken. The ring turns amber below 80% and red below 50%.
The agents
🔭 Watcher
A background agent that continuously scans markets, news, social, and fundamentals for each pillar's watch signals. It surfaces candidates — it doesn't decide.
🔬 Analyst
Triggered when the watcher finds something. It pulls the evidence, drafts a proposed thesis update — which pillars moved and why — and cites its sources. This is also who you talk to on the Ask tab.
🛡️ Supervisor
A quality gate. Before any change touches your theses, the supervisor checks: is the evidence cited? is the reasoning sound? is the pillar change warranted? Approve → committed. Reject → analyst tries again.
Exit plan
Take profit
A target price or condition at which you'd realize gains. Set this when you build the thesis so you don't have to decide in the heat of the moment.
Thesis break
The condition that signals the thesis is invalidated — usually expressed as "if pillar X breaks, I exit." This is your "stop loss" tied to logic, not price.
Time stop
A deadline at which you re-evaluate if the thesis hasn't played out. Prevents money sitting in a stalled position forever.
